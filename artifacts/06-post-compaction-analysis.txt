Interaction read:
- There is a shared seam around compaction/post-compaction lifecycle and context-pressure messages, but the guard and continuation substrate do not decide from the same exact observable.
- The v5.4 guard is tool-loop persistence detection after compaction; continuation is context-pressure detection plus staged delegate release at compaction boundaries.
- Relationship looks cooperative/orthogonal: continuation can intentionally stage work across compaction, while the guard only aborts repeated identical tool outcomes after compaction fails to break a loop.
- No new continuation-specific guard config appears required for the rebase. The driver still needs a three-way merge on run.ts/attempt.ts/openclaw-tools.ts and diagnostic.ts, because those files show textual overlap, but this is merge wiring, not product-design integration.
